Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

MindScape is a mind-mapping app that lets you organise ideas on a canvas, turn ideas into tasks with reminders, get AI suggestions, and share maps with other people. This policy explains what data the app handles, why, and the choices you have. The app is provided by MindScape ("we", "us").

What we collect

  • Account details. Your email address, display name (if you sign in with Google) and a unique user ID. If you register with email and password, the password is handled by Firebase Authentication; we never see or store it.
  • Your content. Maps you create, their titles, the text, colours and positions of ideas, task due dates and completion status, and any images you attach to ideas.
  • Collaboration data. When you invite someone to a map, the app looks up their account by email. For this, we keep a small record linking a verified email address to its user ID, so other signed-in users can find you only if they already know your exact email address.
  • AI suggestions. When you tap "Suggest related ideas", the text of the selected idea is sent to Google's Gemini API through Firebase AI Logic to generate suggestions. Nothing is sent unless you tap that button.
  • App integrity. Firebase App Check, using Google Play Integrity, confirms that requests come from the genuine app. Google may process device integrity signals for this purpose.

Task reminders are scheduled locally on your device. The app does not collect your location, contacts, microphone or advertising ID, and it can only access photos you choose to attach.

How we use it

  • To provide the app: sign you in, store and sync your maps, show shared maps to the people you invite, and generate AI suggestions you request.
  • To keep the service secure and prevent abuse.
  • To respond to your support and privacy requests.

We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your data while your account exists. You can delete your account at any time in the app: open the menu on the home screen and choose Delete account. This permanently deletes your account, your profile, all maps you own and their contents, and removes you from maps shared with you. You can also request deletion by email, see the account deletion page.

Images are removed from your maps immediately, but the image files may remain in our media storage for a while. Email us at basmallamx@gmail.com and we will permanently erase them; we also clear unused images periodically.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your personal data, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to complain to your data protection authority. You can exercise these by emailing basmallamx@gmail.com. We will reply within 30 days.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections and protected by access rules so that only you and the people you invite can read your maps. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password and keep your device up to date.

Children

MindScape is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above, and for significant changes we will notify you in the app.
