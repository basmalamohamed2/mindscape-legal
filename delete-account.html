Delete your MindScape account

Last updated: October 7, 2026

Delete it in the app

This is the fastest way and removes everything immediately.

  1. Open MindScape and go to the home screen.
  2. Tap the menu (three dots) at the top right and choose Delete account.
  3. Type DELETE to confirm. If you signed up with email, enter your password; if you signed in with Google, choose the same Google account when asked.

You need an internet connection to complete this.

Can't open the app?

If you have uninstalled the app or can't sign in, email basmallamx@gmail.com from the address registered to your account with the subject "Delete my MindScape account". We will verify the request and delete your account within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and sign-in record.
  • Your profile (email and display name) and your invitation lookup entry.
  • All maps you own, with their ideas, tasks and image references.
  • Your access to maps other people shared with you (those maps remain for their owners).
  • Scheduled task reminders on the device you delete from.

What may remain

Image files you uploaded may stay in our image hosting (Cloudinary) for a while after deletion. Email basmallamx@gmail.com and we will permanently erase them; we also clear unused images periodically.

See the Privacy Policy for details on how we handle your data.
